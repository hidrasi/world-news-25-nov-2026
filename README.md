# World News — Wednesday 25 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun bounty [Sum up 25 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125).

A 1024×1024 newspaper front page summing up Wednesday 25 November 2026:
Ukrainian drone strikes on Russian oil refineries in the Samara region and
Putin vowing retaliation, a Russian strike killing two in Odesa, the
Australia–Solomon Islands security pact, Asian tech shares sliding, and a
Dutch court keeping binding climate targets — plus the day's other news
and sport.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`, after checking that it is a whole PNG of at most
1 MiB. Run it twice and both runs write the same bytes.

## Stories in the image

- **Lead — Refinery war:** Ukrainian drones struck oil refineries in
  Russia's Samara region overnight, setting plants ablaze; President
  Vladimir Putin said retaliation was "inevitable", despite Donald
  Trump's request to stop the attacks (The Independent, ISW).
- **Ukraine — Odesa:** a Russian strike killed two people and wounded
  three, including a child, in the Black Sea region of Odesa; drones
  also hit a block of flats in Kyiv, injuring two (Reuters).
- **Pacific — Solomons pact:** Australia becomes the Solomon Islands'
  primary security partner under a new pact that rules out foreign
  military bases, a challenge to Chinese influence (AP).
- **Markets — AI slide:** Asian markets fell sharply as investors sold
  AI-linked shares after a Wall Street retreat, days after the rally
  that followed Nvidia's reinstatement of Jensen Huang (AP).
- **Europe — Climate law:** the Hague Court of Appeal refused to
  suspend binding national climate targets while the state's appeal
  proceeds (NOS).
- **Also today:** South Korea's education minister quits over an
  exam-cheating scandal; China opens a corruption probe into Shanghai
  party chief Chen Jining; NATO vows to defend space after reports of
  a Russian nuclear satellite; Nicolas Sarkozy's corruption case goes
  to trial; Donald Trump awards Rudy Giuliani the Medal of Freedom.
- **Sport:** Real Madrid rout PSV Eindhoven 5–1 as Kylian Mbappé
  scores twice; Arsenal held 1–1 by Borussia Dortmund; Manchester
  City draw 2–2 with Napoli (Champions League, Matchday 5).

The hero illustration is an original drawing of a burning refinery in
the Samara region; all headlines and text are set in the page itself,
so the image reads on its own. Every story above happened, or was
first reported, on Wednesday 25 November 2026.
